# METAL SLUG: AWAKENING — 2-Player Co-op Arcade Battle

An authentic recreation of **Metal Slug: Awakening**, featuring:
- **Player 1 (Marco Rossi)**: Spiky blonde hair, white cloth headband, purple/crimson sleeveless high-collar vest, backpack, and leather bracer (matching **Picture 1**).
- **The Awakening Dual Cannon**: Crimson receiver, gold top shroud, double dark steel barrels with gold muzzle bands, side mounted orange canister, and silver elbow pipe (matching **Picture 2**).
- **Commander Allen O'Neil**: Shirtless ripped 8-pack muscular juggernaut, bald head, black beard, and heavy M60 machine gun with long dangling brass ammo belt looping down to his hand (matching **Picture 3**).
- **2-Player Local Co-op**: Play together with Marco & Tarma.
- **4 Action-Packed Stages / Missions**:
  - Mission 1: Jungle Ruins (Boss: Commander Allen O'Neil)
  - Mission 2: Desert Tombs (Driveable SV-001 Slug Tank & Iron Nokana)
  - Mission 3: Frozen Fortress (Arctic Gunship & Snow Bridges)
  - Final Mission: The Jupiter King Apex Core

## Controls
| Action | Player 1 (Marco) | Player 2 (Tarma) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move / Aim Up / Crouch** | <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> | <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> |
| **Dual Cannon / Knife Slash** | <kbd>J</kbd> | <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Z</kbd> |
| **Jump / Enter Slug Tank** | <kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> | <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>X</kbd> |
| **Throw Grenade** | <kbd>L</kbd> | <kbd>.</kbd> or <kbd>C</kbd> |

- **Touchscreen**: On-screen tactical D-pad and action buttons automatically enabled on mobile/tablet (toggle with 📱 button).
- **Dual Gamepad Support**: Plug-and-play for 2 USB / Bluetooth controllers.
- **CRT Monitor Filter**: Vintage tactical monitor scanlines overlay (🖥️ button).
