Before You Ask

Quick Answers

The things players ask us most, answered up front.

How quickly will you reply?+

WhatsApp is usually answered within a few hours during store hours. Emails and contact form messages are answered within one working day. If your question is about an active order, include your order reference so we can help faster.

Can you help me choose the right gear?+

Yes. Tell us your position, playing level, height, and what you're currently using. We'll recommend options that fit your game and budget — no pressure to buy the most expensive item.

Do you take team and academy orders?+

We supply clubs, schools, and academies across the country. Send us your item list, sizes, and quantities, and we'll prepare a bulk quote with a delivery plan for your team.

Can I return or exchange an item?+

Unused items in their original packaging can usually be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Contact us with your order reference and a photo of the item, and we'll guide you through the process.

Do I need an appointment to visit?+

No appointment is needed. Drop in during opening hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. For team fittings or large orders, message us first so we can set aside time and stock for you.
