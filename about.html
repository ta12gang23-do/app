Who We Are

Gear That Earns Its Place in Your Bag

HockeyPro Store started with a simple observation: too many players were buying expensive gear that did not fit the way they played. A stick that felt perfect in the shop could feel heavy in the second half. A kit that looked the part could fall apart in a season.

So we built a different kind of hockey store — one that puts the player first. We test what we sell, we explain the difference between products honestly, and we help you choose gear that matches your position, your level, and your budget.

Today we supply sticks, protective gear, shoes, and match-day essentials to individual players, school teams, and academies across the country. Every product in our collection is chosen for one reason: it performs when it matters.

  • Hand-picked gear that we would use ourselves
  • Straight advice from people who play the game
  • Fair pricing for individuals, teams, and academies
