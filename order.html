Common Questions

Before You Order

Quick answers to the things players ask us most.

How do I place an order?+

Fill in the order form above and submit it. We will contact you on WhatsApp or by phone to confirm the product, delivery address, and payment method. Your order is only confirmed after we speak with you.

How long does delivery take?+

Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days and costs Rs. 250. Express delivery takes 1–2 working days and costs Rs. 600. Standard delivery is free on orders of Rs. 10,000 or more.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We offer cash on delivery and bank transfer. No online payment is taken on this page — we share the relevant details after confirming your order.

Can I order for a team or academy?+

Yes. We regularly supply clubs, schools, and academies. Message us on WhatsApp with the items and quantities you need, and we will prepare a bulk quote for you.

Can I change or cancel my order?+

Contact us as soon as possible after submitting your request. We can usually adjust or cancel an order before it is dispatched.
